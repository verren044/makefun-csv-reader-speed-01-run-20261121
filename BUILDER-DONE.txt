Entry name: fast broken-CSV reader (csv-reader 1.0.0 drop-in)

Approach
--------
solve.js (ES module, stdlib only, no network, deterministic) replaces the
reference's char-at-a-time parse with native indexOf/slice scanning:
- Unquoted field: indexOf(',') + indexOf('\n') find the delimiter, one
  slice is the field; CRLF recognised by the char before '\n'. Only a
  stray CR inside a field falls back to a char loop.
- Quoted field: indexOf('"') finds the closing/doubled quote, slices
  accumulate; only segments with a line break pay for CR->LF transform
  and line recount.
- BOM, empty lines, stray-CR-only lines, short/long rows, and all error
  records (stray_cr, unterminated_quote, quote_junk, short_row, long_row)
  in exact reference order. main.ts is the baseline driver verbatim.
